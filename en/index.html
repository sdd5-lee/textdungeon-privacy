TextDungeon Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

This is a translation of the Korean original. If the two differ, the Korean version prevails.

Kyungjae Lee (the "Developer") values the personal information of everyone who uses the TextDungeon app (the "App") and complies with the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea and other applicable laws. This policy explains what information the App sends, to whom, and why.

1. Information the App handles

  • You can use the App without signing in. The App does not ask you to enter information such as your name or phone number.
  • If you do not sign in, your game progress, currencies (gems and gold), unlocks and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The App signs in with your Google account only if you turn on "Link Google Account" in Settings yourself. In that case, your Google account's email address, name, profile picture URL and account identifier are stored in Firebase Authentication, and your game progress, currencies and unlocks are stored in Cloud Firestore. This information is used only to back up your records and let you continue on other devices.
  • The Developer does not run its own servers. The information above is kept in Google's Firebase.
  • The App uses the following permissions: internet access, network state, advertising ID and ad measurement (AdMob), and Google Play billing. It also includes basic permissions that the ad and billing features need (such as keeping the device awake during a task). It does not use location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone permissions.

4. Retention and deletion

  • If you linked a Google account, your account information and the game records stored in the cloud are kept until you request deletion. When you request it, they are deleted without delay (section 7). The Developer keeps no information about users who did not link an account.
  • Game records and settings stored on your device are deleted when you uninstall the App or clear the App's data in your device settings. Paid purchase records stay with your Google Play account, so you can get them back with "Restore Purchases" after reinstalling.
  • Information processed by third-party services is kept and deleted under each company's policy.

5. Personalized ads and how to opt out

AdMob may use your advertising ID to show ads based on your interests. If you do not want this, you can delete or reset your advertising ID in your device's Settings → Privacy (Security and privacy on Samsung devices) → Ads. After you delete the advertising ID you may still see ads, but they will not be personalized.

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland see an ad consent form the first time they open the App. You can change your choice at any time in the App's Settings → Ad privacy settings.

6. Children's information

The App is intended for users aged 16 and over and is not a service directed at children. If you learn that a child's information has been processed, please let us know at the contact below.

7. Your rights

You can delete the information stored on your device at any time (by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App) and delete your advertising ID. You can ask each company to show or delete the information its service has processed. Please send questions or requests to the contact below.

Deleting your account and cloud records: If you sign out in "Link Google Account" in Settings, that device stops uploading records. To delete the account information and cloud game records already stored, email the contact in section 9 from the email address of the linked Google account. We will check the request and delete everything without delay. Records left on your device are not deleted.
