Delete your TextDungeon account and data

Developer: Kyungjae Lee · Last updated: November 12, 2026

This is a translation of the Korean original. If the two differ, the Korean version prevails.

You can use TextDungeon (the "App") without signing in. This page explains how users who signed in with "Link Google Account" in Settings can delete their account and the records backed up to the cloud.

1. Delete in the App

  1. Open Settings from the App's main screen.
  2. On the "Account" tab, tap "Link Google Account".
  3. Tap "Delete account and cloud records" at the bottom, then tap "Delete" in the confirmation window.
  4. To confirm it is you, choose the linked Google account once more. The data is deleted right away.

2. Request without the App

If you have already uninstalled the App or cannot delete in the App, send an email from the email address of the linked Google account to the address below.

  • To: rudwo6285@gmail.com
  • Subject: TextDungeon account deletion request

To confirm your identity, if the sending address is different from the linked Google account, we may ask you to send it again from that account. We will delete your data within 10 days of receiving the request and tell you the result by email.

3. Data that is deleted and data that remains

DataWhat happens
Google account information (email address, name, profile picture URL, account identifier)Deleted
Game records backed up to the cloud (progress, currencies, unlocks)Deleted
Game records stored on your phoneRemain. To delete them, uninstall the App or clear the App's data in your device settings.
Google Play purchase historyRemains. It is recorded in your Google Play account, and you can get your purchases back with "Restore Purchases" after reinstalling.
Information processed by ads (AdMob)Under Google's policy. You can delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

4. Retention period

We do not keep deleted account information or cloud records anywhere else. Because the records are stored in Google's Firebase, it may take some time, under Google's policy, until they are completely removed from Google's backup systems.

For more details, see the Privacy Policy.
